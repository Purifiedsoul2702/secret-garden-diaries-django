YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy, simply.

You can browse all public guides without an account. Accounts store your username, email, securely hashed password, and the garden entries you choose to save. Email is used for password recovery.

We use essential session, security and language-preference cookies. This project includes no advertising, tracking analytics or third-party fonts. Photographs are served locally.

Login protection records attempted usernames and connection IP addresses. Registration and password-reset limits use a hashed connection identifier. Operators should run the documented daily cleanup to retain these security records for no longer than seven days.

If weather is enabled, the server sends the selected area's public coordinates to Open-Meteo. Your account details and garden notes are not sent. Your hosting and email providers process data needed to operate the service.

Use My Garden to export your records. You can delete individual entries or delete your account and its garden records. Copies may remain in backups until the operator's backup retention period ends.

Before public launch the site operator must add their contact address, hosting/email provider names and backup retention period here. This supplied notice describes the application's behaviour and is not a completed legal policy.
