OUR ROOTS

A garden for everyone.

Secret Garden Diaries is a free guide to growing a little more green in Indian homes.

Useful knowledge, honestly shared

Our starter library combines original educational summaries with links to agricultural references. Regional calendars are broad draft guidance until locally reviewed; growing success depends on variety, elevation, weather and your growing space. A reference is not an endorsement of this website.

Five languages, one shared curiosity

Plant names, summaries, core care guidance and main navigation are available in English, Hindi, Marathi, Tamil and Bangla. Translations are editorial drafts. Some detailed guides and account controls currently fall back to English, clearly labelled where article content is missing.
